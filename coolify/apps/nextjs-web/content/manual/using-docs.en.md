Operation code **DC01**. This page explains how to use the Manuals app itself. It is available to all users (no permission required).

## What this app does

You can read the user manuals for every app in the system without leaving the system. Manuals are available in three languages: **日本語 (Japanese), English, and 中文 (Chinese)**.

- The left sidebar has two sections: **Operations** (step-by-step instructions and what each input field means, grouped by category) and **Process** (how the business flows, and which app is used at each stage).
- If you are new to the system, start with the [Start Manual](/manual/en/start).

## How to open

- Click **マニュアル (Manuals)** on the home screen or in the launcher, or search for `DC01` (the top-right search box is desktop only; on a phone use the search inside the launcher).
- Where an input field has a **?** next to it, you can read the explanation right there. Choose **Read more** to open the full description of that field.

## Switching languages

- Click **日本語 / English / 中文** at the top of the sidebar (on mobile, the bar at the top of the page) to switch the display language.
- The language is kept as you move between pages. Links inside the manuals also lead to pages in the same language.

## Search

- Click the **Search** button in the sidebar, or press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on Windows) to open the search window.
- Type a keyword to search the full text of all manuals. Click a result to jump to that page.
- Search covers the manuals in the currently displayed language.

## Enlarging images

- Press a screenshot in the text to open it full screen.
- Zoom in with the mouse wheel, a pinch, or a double-click, and drag to move it. You can also use the 「拡大」 (Zoom in), 「縮小」 (Zoom out) and 「元のサイズに戻す」 (Reset zoom) buttons at the top right of the image.
- To close it, press the × at the top right or the Esc key.

## Watching videos

- Some pages (such as the process flows) have a recording of the actual operation. A 「**Steps**」 list appears below the video; press an item to jump to that scene. The videos were recorded with the Japanese interface.
- In environments with no video, the video frame is not shown at all. The pages are written so that the pictures and text alone are enough.

## Back to the app

- Press 「**Back to the app**」 in the sidebar (the menu on mobile) to return to the home screen of the business app.

## FAQ

- **A screen described in a manual is missing** … Some apps may not be published in every environment (production / development). Apps you have no permission for are also hidden from your home screen.
- **The manual differs from the actual screen** … Screens can change ahead of the manuals when the system is updated. Please report anything you notice to an administrator.
