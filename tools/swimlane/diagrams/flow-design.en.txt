/line/

[role_cust: Ask about a shape] <block_neutral>
label: Inquiry;
desc: A special shape is asked for or a new product is being studied, so a drawing is needed.;
if (Trigger) is (At quote) than #blue
  [role_sales: From a quote] <block_apply>
  label: At quote;
  props: APP_SA03,DR;
  desc: Opened from "Raise design request" on the quote. Trigger = at quote and that quote are filled in.;
elseif (At order) than #blue
  [role_sales: From order line] <block_apply>
  label: At order;
  props: APP_SA05,DR;
  desc: Opened from "Raise design request" on the order line. Trigger = at order and that order line are filled in.;
elseif (Standalone) than #blue
  [role_sales: Raise standalone] <block_apply>
  label: Standalone;
  props: APP_SA06,DR;
  desc: A request tied to neither a quote nor an order. It can also be raised from New on the list, from the product master, or from "No price list" on a quote.;
endif
[role_system: New or revision?] <block_system>
label: Request kind;
desc: Revision if the product × ordering-customer series already has a version, otherwise New. It can be switched by hand.;
if (Request kind) is (Revision) than #blue
  [role_sales: Base and reason] <block_apply>
  label: Revision;
  props: APP_SA06;
  desc: Enter the version to start from (empty = latest) and the reason for the change (required).;
elseif (New) than #blue
endif
[role_sales: Save draft] <block_apply>
label: Draft;
props: APP_SA06,DR;
desc: A request number (DSG-) is assigned and it becomes Draft. The assignee is notified. While in draft the content can be edited.;
[role_sales: Request approval] <block_apply>
label: Approval request;
props: APP_SA06;
desc: It becomes Pending approval and the first-step approvers in Approval settings (MS0B) are notified.;
if (Decision) is (Send back) than #red
  [role_appr: Send back] <block_reject>
  label: Sent back;
  props: APP_CM01;
  desc: The status becomes Sent back.;
  [role_sales: Fix and resubmit] <block_apply>
  label: Resubmit;
  props: APP_SA06;
  desc: Fix the content and press Request approval again.;
  [loop]
else
  [role_appr: Approve request] <block_approve>
  label: Approval;
  props: APP_CM01;
  desc: Approved step by step; after the last step it becomes Not started.;
endif
[role_system: Notify assignee] <block_system>
label: Not started;
desc: The assignee receives a "please start" notification.;
if (Still needed?) is (No longer) than #red
  [role_sales: Cancel request] <block_end>
  label: Cancelled;
  props: APP_SA06,DR;
  desc: Enter a reason and cancel. Possible from any status before Completed; a pending approval request is withdrawn too. The flow ends here.;
elseif (Continue) than #blue
endif
[role_design: Start work] <block_apply>
label: Start;
props: APP_SA06,DR;
desc: It becomes In progress and the requester is notified. Assignee and product can still be changed from the ⋯ menu.;
[role_design: Attach work files] <block_neutral>
label: Work files;
props: APP_SA06;
desc: Upload notes and drafts on the Files tab (optional). Only possible while Not started or In progress.;
[role_design: Register drawing] <block_apply>
label: Register drawing;
props: APP_PD06,DV;
desc: Opened from "Register in Drawing" on the request, with product and ordering customer fixed to the request. A draft version is created with the next number of the series.;
[role_design: Complete request] <block_apply>
label: Completed;
props: APP_SA06,DR;
desc: Only possible when at least one version came from this request. It becomes Completed; the requester (and, at quote, the quote's sales rep) is notified.;
if (Review) is (Redraw) than #red
  [role_sales: Reopen request] <block_reject>
  label: Rework;
  props: APP_SA06;
  desc: Back to In progress and the completion date is cleared. Approval is not taken again. The assignee is notified.;
  [role_design: Redraw, complete] <block_apply>
  label: Re-complete;
  props: APP_PD06,APP_SA06;
  desc: Register a new version and press Complete again. The earlier version stays.;
  [loop]
else
endif
[role_design: Submit version] <block_apply>
label: Confirm version;
props: APP_PD06,DV;
desc: Check the draft version and press Confirm or Request approval. Until confirmed, spec and files can be edited.;
if (Version steps) is (Set up) than #blue
  if (Decision) is (Send back) than #red
    [role_appr: Send back] <block_reject>
    label: Sent back;
    props: APP_CM01;
    desc: The version becomes Sent back.;
    [role_design: Fix and resubmit] <block_apply>
    label: Resubmit;
    props: APP_PD06;
    desc: Fix spec or files and press Request approval again.;
    [loop]
  else
    [role_appr: Approve version] <block_approve>
    label: Approval;
    props: APP_CM01;
    desc: After the steps set up for "Drawing version" in Approval settings it becomes Confirmed.;
  endif
elseif (None) than #blue
  [role_design: Confirm directly] <block_approve>
  label: Confirmed;
  props: APP_PD06;
  desc: With no steps set up, one press of Confirm confirms it.;
endif
[role_system: Switch latest] <block_system>
label: Latest drawing;
desc: "Latest" of the series moves to this version. Other customers' series are untouched. The product master reads its spec from this version.;
if (WO drawing) is (Pin) than #purple
  [role_prod: Pin a version] <block_apply>
  label: Pin;
  props: APP_PD02,WO;
  desc: Only confirmed versions can be chosen. Later revisions do not change what the floor sees, and a pinned version can no longer be edited.;
elseif (Don't pin) than #purple
  [role_system: Show the latest] <block_system>
  label: Automatic;
  desc: Latest version of the ordering customer's series, otherwise of the generic series. Another customer's series is never used.;
endif
