/line/

if (Cause) is (Receipt) than #blue
  [role_system: Receive material] <block_system>
  label: Receipt;
  desc: Registering a material receipt (PU03) or a receipt against a material purchase order adds material stock at the receiving site.;
elseif (Allocation) than #blue
  [role_system: Reserve stock] <block_system>
  label: Reserve;
  desc: A stock check on an order line reserves free product stock; approving a manufacture work order reserves its material. Material may be reserved beyond what is on hand — that is the shortage signal.;
elseif (WO completed) than #blue
  [role_system: Put away output] <block_system>
  label: WO done;
  desc: When every step is completed, good pieces go into stock with the lot number and semi-finished ones as semi-finished. Material reservations are released and the used material is issued (never more than the ledger holds).;
elseif (Shipment) than #blue
  if (Stock) is (Short) than #orange
    [role_system: Ship into minus] <block_reject>
    label: Shortage;
    desc: A shortage never blocks shipping. The missing quantity is issued anyway as "shipped without stock" and the stock goes negative.;
  elseif (Enough) than
    [role_system: Issue & release] <block_system>
    label: Shipment;
    desc: Shipping a dispatch delivery order issues the products and releases their reservation. A stock-storage delivery order puts stock in instead.;
  endif
elseif (Return) than #blue
  [role_system: Book the return] <block_system>
  label: Return;
  desc: Recording a return on a shipped delivery order puts the pieces back into stock, up to the quantity shipped.;
elseif (Cancellation) than #blue
  [role_system: Drop reservation] <block_system>
  label: Release;
  desc: Cancelling a work order, order line or order acceptance releases the reservations it held.;
elseif (Outsourcing) than #blue
  [role_system: Move partner stock] <block_system>
  label: Outsource;
  desc: Sending to a subcontractor increases stock held by a partner; the return decreases it. It is never counted as own stock.;
elseif (Regrind) than #blue
  [role_system: Receive tools] <block_system>
  label: Regrind;
  desc: Completing the regrind product receipt books the customer's tools as customer-owned stock. Own stock does not change.;
endif
[role_system: Record movement] <block_system>
label: Slip;
props: MV;
desc: Every time stock moves, one stock movement slip is kept with its cause and source document. Nobody can create one by hand.;
[role_prod: Check stock] <block_neutral>
label: Check;
props: APP_ST02;
desc: Look by place in Stock overview; items, WIP and reservations in Inventory (ST01); what moved in Stock movement (ST04).;
[role_prod: Check coverage] <block_neutral>
label: Coverage;
props: APP_ST03;
desc: Puts one item's past movements and planned ones on a single timeline to show when it runs short.;
if (Outlook) is (Short) than #orange
  [role_prod: Hand to purchasing] <block_wait>
  label: Replenish;
  desc: Material that will run short is covered by a purchase request and a material purchase order.;
elseif (Enough) than
endif
if (What to fix) is (Location) than #purple
  [role_prod: Transfer stock] <block_apply>
  label: Transfer;
  props: APP_ST01,MV;
  desc: Move to another shelf, storage location or site. Only unreserved stock can move. The issue at the source and the receipt at the target sit on one slip.;
elseif (In / out) than #purple
  [role_admin: Set movement types] <block_neutral>
  label: Types;
  props: APP_ST09;
  desc: Defines the direction (in / out / transfer) and whether source and target are required. Types are never deleted, only disabled.;
  [role_prod: Post manual move] <block_apply>
  label: Manual;
  props: APP_ST06;
  desc: Pick a movement type and enter item and quantity. Each registration creates one slip with the cause "Manual".;
  if (Source stock) is (Short) than #red
    [role_prod: Cannot post] <block_reject>
    label: Shortage;
    desc: Blocked when the source does not hold enough. Fix the quantity or the source.;
  elseif (Enough) than
    [role_system: Update stock] <block_system>
    label: Posted;
    props: MV;
    desc: A transfer records the issue first, then the receipt. To undo a mistake, post the opposite direction.;
  endif
elseif (Count wrong) than #purple
  [role_prod: Create stocktake] <block_apply>
  label: Create;
  props: APP_ST05,STK;
  desc: Choose a site (and optionally a storage location); every bucket there becomes a line, including empty ones. Status: Draft.;
  [role_prod: Count and record] <block_apply>
  label: Count;
  props: APP_ST05;
  desc: Enter the counted quantities and save. The first save moves it to Counting; you can save as often as you like.;
  if (Next) is (Cancel) than #red
    [role_prod: Cancel stocktake] <block_end>
    label: Cancel;
    desc: Possible while Draft or Counting. If approval is pending, the request is withdrawn too.;
  elseif (Submit) than #blue
    [role_prod: Submit] <block_apply>
    label: Submit;
    props: APP_ST05;
    desc: Possible once at least one line is counted.;
    if (Approval flow) is (Has steps) than #blue
      if (Decision) is (Send back) than #red
        [role_prod: Recount, resubmit] <block_reject>
        label: Sent back;
        desc: Sent back with a reason, the counts become editable again.;
        [loop]
      else
        [role_appr: Approve stocktake] <block_approve>
        label: Approve;
        props: APP_ST05;
        desc: Counts are locked while approval is pending. Once every step approves, it is confirmed.;
      endif
    elseif (No steps) than
    endif
    [role_system: Post differences] <block_system>
    label: Confirm;
    props: MV;
    desc: Compares the counts with stock at the moment of confirmation and posts an "Adjustment" slip for the differences only; no difference, no slip. Status: Confirmed — cannot be undone.;
  endif
endif
