[role_support: Check stock] <block_apply>
label: Stock check;
props: APP_SA05,OL;
desc: Checks product stock for an order line in "Confirmed". Lines already in production and regrind lines are excluded.;
[role_system: Reserve stock] <block_system>
label: Allocate;
desc: Reserves stock up to the available quantity and shows the rest as a shortage. The shortage is made by a Manufacture work order.;
[role_prod: Review unplanned] <block_neutral>
label: Pending;
props: APP_PD05;
desc: Lines with an unplanned quantity (ordered − planned) are listed by delivery date. "Create work order" opens the form with that quantity filled in.;
if (How) is (Copy previous) than #purple
  [role_prod: Copy previous WO] <block_apply>
  label: Copy;
  props: APP_PD02,WO;
  desc: Pick the target order line; a draft is created with the steps, execution locations and inspection sheets. A warning shows if a newer version exists.;
elseif (For stock) than #purple
  [role_prod: Pick product] <block_apply>
  label: For stock;
  props: APP_PD02;
  desc: A standalone work order with no order line. Only Manufacture is allowed; it builds up stock.;
else
  [role_prod: Allocate lines] <block_apply>
  label: Allocation;
  props: APP_PD02;
  desc: Pick order lines and quantities. The limit is what is left to allocate (ordered − other work orders).;
  if (Allocation) is (Part only) than #purple
    [role_prod: Plan rest later] <block_wait>
    label: Split;
    desc: The rest stays under "Not planned" in Pending work orders and is planned by another work order.;

    [role_prod: Merge lines] <block_apply>
    label: Merge;
    desc: Other lines of the same product go into one work order (one lot). Not allowed for From stock or Regrind.;

  endif
endif
