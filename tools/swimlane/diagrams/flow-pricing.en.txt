/line/

if (Item) is (Product) than #blue
  [role_sales: Create estimate] <block_apply>
  label: Estimate;
  props: APP_SA01;
  desc: Choose the tool type and enter dimensions, material (material type, diameter, surface finish) and machining conditions. The estimated unit price is recalculated on every input.;
  if (Material price) is (Automatic) than #blue
    [role_system: Get ref. price] <block_system>
    label: Ref. price;
    desc: Derives ¥/1000mm from purchase history of the same material type, diameter and surface finish, per the settings (latest / highest / average and look-back months). With no history the material type's default price is used and a "Default price" badge appears.;
  elseif (Cylinder) than #purple
    [role_sales: Enter bar price] <block_apply>
    label: Manual;
    props: APP_SA01;
    desc: For the cylinder tool type the reference price is not used; enter the material price (¥/pc) by hand.;
  endif
  if (Adjust price) is (Adjust) than #purple
    [role_sales: Edit ref. price] <block_apply>
    label: Custom;
    props: APP_SA01;
    desc: If the market differs, re-enter the reference price with "Edit price". A "Custom" badge appears; "Back to policy value" restores the automatic price.;
  elseif (Keep) than
  endif
  [role_sales: Save as draft] <block_apply>
  label: Save;
  props: APP_SA01,EST;
  desc: A name is required. It gets an EST- number and becomes "Draft". The result at save time is recorded and the content can no longer be edited.;
  if (Content) is (Redo) than #purple
    [role_sales: Duplicate & redo] <block_apply>
    label: Duplicate;
    props: APP_SA01;
    desc: Opens the form with the same content and "(redo)" added to the name. Saving creates a draft with a new number. Any status can be duplicated.;
    [loop]
  elseif (Final) than
  endif
  if (Product) is (Not set) than #orange
    [role_sales: Link a product] <block_apply>
    label: Link;
    props: APP_SA01;
    desc: Only estimates with a product can be chosen in a price list. While Draft or Confirmed the link can be changed or removed.;
  elseif (Set) than
  endif
  [role_sales: Confirm] <block_approve>
  label: Confirm;
  props: APP_SA01,EST;
  desc: Only a Draft can be confirmed. It is recalculated with the current settings, recorded, and becomes "Confirmed".;
elseif (Regrind) than #purple
  [role_admin: Set std. price] <block_apply>
  label: Std. price;
  props: APP_MS0H,RGI;
  desc: The regrind service holds a standard price (list price) on the regrind item. No price estimate is used. The standard price cannot be changed from the price list screen.;
endif
if (Price list) is (Create) than #blue
  [role_sales: Create price list] <block_apply>
  label: Price list;
  props: APP_SA02,PL;
  desc: One per customer × item, with base price, valid period and quantity tiers per order type. Products take Production / Test / Sample / Other, regrind items only Regrind. Test and Sample need an end date.;
  if (Pair) is (Exists) than #orange
    [role_sales: Edit existing one] <block_reject>
    label: Duplicate;
    props: APP_SA02;
    desc: The same customer × item already exists, so it cannot be saved. Open the existing price list and add order types or prices by editing it.;
  elseif (New) than
  endif
  if (Base price) is (Estimate) than #blue
    [role_system: Lock the estimate] <block_system>
    label: Lock;
    props: EST;
    desc: The estimated unit price of a confirmed estimate for the same product becomes the base price. An estimate used for the first time becomes "Registered" and its product link can no longer change.;
  elseif (Manual) than #purple
    [role_sales: Enter base price] <block_apply>
    label: Manual;
    props: APP_SA02;
    desc: Enter the base price by hand with "Use custom price". The estimate source shows "Manual". Custom prices can also be set per tier.;
  endif
  if (Discount) is (Add) than #purple
    [role_sales: Add discount rule] <block_apply>
    label: Discount;
    props: APP_SA02,PL;
    desc: Set a rate (%) or amount (¥/pc) with quantity and period conditions. If several match, the largest discount is used.;
  elseif (None) than
  endif
elseif (Regrind at std.) than #gray
endif
[role_sales: Create quote] <block_apply>
label: Quote;
props: APP_SA03,QOT;
desc: Three entry points: New, "Create quote" on a price list, or duplicating a quote. Choosing customer, item, order type and quantity fills unit price, discount and amount automatically (no manual entry).;
if (Unit price) is (Found) than #blue
  [role_system: Look up the price] <block_system>
  label: Unit price;
  desc: From an active price list and active order type whose valid period covers today, the tier matching the quantity gives the unit price (base price × multiplier, or custom) and the discount. A regrind item with no matching price list uses its standard price.;
elseif (Not found) than #red
  [role_sales: Fix the price list] <block_reject>
  label: No price;
  props: APP_SA02;
  desc: A product line with no matching price list (missing, inactive or out of period), or a price list without a tier for the quantity, cannot be saved. Prepare the estimate and price list, or add the tier.;
  [loop]
endif
[role_sales: Save as draft] <block_apply>
label: Save;
props: APP_SA03,QOT;
desc: It gets a QOT- number and becomes "Draft". Unit price, discount and tax are frozen at save time. A draft can be edited; saving again re-reads the current price list.;
if (Drawing) is (None) than #purple
  [role_sales: Raise design req.] <block_apply>
  label: Design req.;
  props: APP_SA06,DR;
  desc: Raising it from the quote's menu carries the quote over. It runs in parallel with the quote (not possible from an expired quote).;
elseif (Exists) than
endif
[role_sales: Issue] <block_approve>
label: Issue;
props: APP_SA03,QOT;
desc: A valid-until date is required and must be today or later. It becomes "Issued"; after that it can be neither edited nor un-issued.;
[role_system: Generate PDF] <block_system>
label: PDF;
props: QOT;
desc: The quote PDF is generated and stored on issue. Only issued quotes have a PDF.;
if (PDF) is (Failed) than #orange
  [role_sales: Regenerate PDF] <block_reject>
  label: Regenerate;
  props: APP_SA03;
  desc: The quote is already issued. Regenerate from the PDF tab.;
elseif (OK) than
endif
[role_sales: Send PDF to client] <block_apply>
label: Submit;
props: APP_SA03;
desc: Download it from the PDF tab and send it by email or on paper. The "email to customer" checkbox in the issue dialog does not send anything automatically.;
[role_cust: Receive the quote] <block_neutral>
label: Receive;
desc: Reviews the quote. There is no operation to record the reply on the quote.;
if (Reply) is (Order) than #blue
  [role_sales: Go to order] <block_apply>
  label: To order;
  props: APP_SA04,OA;
  desc: When an order acceptance names this quote, it appears under "To" on the quote. The order flow starts here.;
elseif (Declined/none) than #gray
  [role_system: Show as expired] <block_end>
  label: Expired;
  props: QOT;
  desc: An issued quote past its valid-until date is shown as "Expired" automatically (not stored as a status).;
elseif (Revise) than #purple
  [role_sales: Duplicate, reissue] <block_apply>
  label: Duplicate;
  props: APP_SA03,QOT;
  desc: An issued quote cannot be changed, so duplicate it into a new draft and start again from step 15.;
endif
