/line/

[role_prod: Spot a shortage] <block_neutral>
label: Shortage;
props: APP_PD02;
desc: Choosing the material on a work order compares it with stock; if it is short with nothing expected, "No incoming receipts" is shown. Stock & requirements (ST03) shows it too. No purchase request is created automatically.;
if (Sourcing) is (With a PO) than #blue
  if (Start) is (From a request) than #blue
    [role_prod: Create request] <block_apply>
    label: Request;
    props: APP_PU01,PR;
    desc: Enter material, receiving site, quantity, requested date and the reason. No price or supplier. It becomes Draft.;
    [role_prod: Request approval] <block_apply>
    label: Approval req.;
    props: APP_PU01;
    desc: Not possible unless a flow exists in Approval settings (MS0B). It becomes Pending approval and the first step's approval group is notified.;
    if (Decision) is (Send back) than #red
      [role_prod: Fix and resubmit] <block_reject>
      label: Sent back;
      props: APP_PU01;
      desc: The approver sends it back with a reason and it becomes Sent back. The requester fixes it and requests approval again without recreating it.;
      [loop]
    elseif (Approve) than #blue
      [role_appr: Approve request] <block_approve>
      label: Approve;
      props: APP_CM01;
      desc: With several steps it becomes Approved only after the last one; in between it stays Pending approval and moves to the next step.;
    endif
    if (Next) is (Withdraw) than #red
      [role_prod: Cancel request] <block_end>
      label: Cancelled;
      props: APP_PU01;
      desc: Before conversion to a PO (Draft, Pending approval, Approved, Sent back) it can be cancelled with a reason. The flow ends here.;
    elseif (Convert) than #blue
      [role_purch: Convert to PO] <block_apply>
      label: Convert;
      props: APP_PU01,PO;
      desc: Pick a supplier and convert. A material purchase order (Draft, unit prices 0) is created with the lines carried over, and the request becomes Ordered.;
    endif
  elseif (Create directly) than #purple
    if (Method) is (AI read) than #purple
      [role_purch: Read supplier doc] <block_apply>
      label: AI read;
      props: APP_PU02;
      desc: Upload the supplier's quote, order acceptance or PO copy; the supplier and materials are matched. Check each line (matched / guessed / unresolved) and apply it to the form. If it cannot be read, enter by hand.;
    elseif (By hand) than
    endif
  endif
  [role_purch: Complete the PO] <block_apply>
  label: PO;
  props: APP_PU02,PO;
  desc: Enter supplier, quantity, unit price and expected date, then save. Amounts are calculated. It can be edited only while Draft.;
  [role_purch: Request approval] <block_apply>
  label: Approval req.;
  props: APP_PU02;
  desc: Not possible without an approval flow. It becomes Pending approval and the approval group is notified.;
  if (Decision) is (Send back) than #red
    [role_purch: Fix and resubmit] <block_reject>
    label: Sent back;
    props: APP_PU02;
    desc: The approver sends it back with a reason and the PO returns to Draft. Fix it and request approval again.;
    [loop]
  elseif (Approve) than #blue
    [role_appr: Approve PO] <block_approve>
    label: Approve;
    props: APP_CM01;
    desc: Approved after all steps. From here supporting documents (order copies, etc.) can be attached.;
  endif
  if (Before order) is (Withdraw) than #red
    [role_purch: Cancel PO] <block_end>
    label: Cancelled;
    props: APP_PU02;
    desc: Only before ordering (Draft, Pending approval, Approved) can it be cancelled with a reason. The flow ends here.;
  elseif (Order) than #blue
  endif
  [role_purch: Place the order] <block_apply>
  label: Order;
  props: APP_PU02;
  desc: It becomes Ordered; an empty order date is set to today. The open quantity shows as incoming in Stock & requirements and in the work order's material hint. An ordered PO cannot be cancelled.;
  [role_supplier: Deliver material] <block_neutral>
  label: Delivery;
  desc: The material arrives with a delivery note — not always all at once.;
  if (Arrival) is (All) than #blue
    [role_system: Receive the rest] <block_system>
    label: Received;
    props: APP_PU02,MR;
    desc: When receiving presses "Received", the whole open quantity is received and a material receipt is recorded per line. The PO becomes Received and the requester and creator are notified.;
  elseif (Part) than #orange
    [role_recv: Record partial] <block_apply>
    label: Partial;
    props: APP_PU02,MR;
    desc: Receive only what arrived, per line. Receiving more than ordered is refused. When the totals are complete the PO becomes Received automatically.;
    [loop]
  elseif (Rest not coming) than #orange
    [role_purch: Close short] <block_end>
    label: Close short;
    props: APP_PU02;
    desc: When the supplier cannot send the rest, close the PO as Received with a reason. The missing quantity never enters stock and drops out of the incoming quantity.;
  endif
elseif (Without a PO) than #purple
  [role_supplier: Deliver material] <block_neutral>
  label: Delivery;
  desc: Material bought without a purchase order (direct purchase, brought in) arrives.;
  if (Entry) is (One by one) than #purple
    [role_recv: Register receipt] <block_apply>
    label: Direct;
    props: APP_PU03,MR;
    desc: Enter material, supplier, receiving site, received date and quantity, then register. The unit is fixed to the material master's unit. It cannot be edited afterwards.;
  elseif (From note) than #purple
    [role_system: Read delivery note] <block_system>
    label: AI read;
    props: APP_PU03;
    desc: Receiving uploads the delivery note (PDF/image) and AI matches the supplier and each line's material. A matching material code settles it; names are matched step by step.;
    [role_recv: Check the lines] <block_apply>
    label: Check;
    props: APP_PU03;
    desc: Exclude lines for another plant or accessories with "Do not register this line". A line without a resolved material cannot be registered (nothing is created on its own).;
    [role_recv: Register all] <block_apply>
    label: Register;
    props: APP_PU03,MR;
    desc: The selected lines are registered in one transaction; if anything fails, all are rolled back. Corrected material and supplier spellings are learned for next time.;
  endif
endif
[role_system: Put into stock] <block_system>
label: Stock in;
desc: The receiving site's material stock rises by the received quantity and a stock movement is recorded. With no site, it goes to "no site" stock.;
[role_recv: Attach documents] <block_apply>
label: Documents;
props: APP_PU03;
desc: Optionally attach delivery-note copies to the receipt and order copies to the PO (from Approved on). The AI-read original is attached automatically; if that fails, the registration still stands.;
if (Outsourced step) is (Yes) than #blue
  [role_prod: Set subcontractor] <block_apply>
  label: Outsource;
  props: APP_PD02,WO;
  desc: On the work order step choose Outsourced as the execution location and pick the subcontractor. No separate outsource-order document is created.;
  [role_prod: Check outsourcing] <block_neutral>
  label: Outsource orders;
  props: APP_PU04;
  desc: Outsourced steps are listed (view only). Filter by subcontractor or status and open the step from a row.;
  [role_prod: Enter dates] <block_apply>
  label: Dates;
  props: APP_PD02;
  desc: In the step's "Outsource schedule", enter the requested, expected and received dates and the cost, and save; the list reflects them.;
elseif (No) than
endif
