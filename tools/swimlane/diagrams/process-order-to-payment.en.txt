[role_cust: Send a purchase order] <block_neutral>
label: Order;
desc: The order arrives by email, fax or PDF.;
[role_sales: Import the order] <block_apply>
label: Order acceptance;
props: OA;
desc: Customer, items and quantities are read automatically. Check the content and the unit prices (matched against the price list).;
[role_sales: Request approval] <block_apply>
label: Request;
if (Approved?) is (Sent back) than #red
  [role_sales: Fix and resubmit] <block_reject>
  label: Sent back;
  [loop]
else
  [role_appr: Approve the order] <block_approve>
  label: Approve;
endif
[role_sales: Confirm the order] <block_apply>
label: Confirm;
props: OL;
desc: One order line is created per row, and its number and amount are fixed.;
[role_sales: Create work order] <block_apply>
label: Work order;
props: WO;
desc: Split into what can come from stock (from stock) and what must be made (manufacture). For manufacture, lay out the steps and choose the material.;
if (Material) is (Short) than #orange
  [role_prod: Request material] <block_apply>
  label: Request;
  props: PR;
  [role_appr: Approve the request] <block_approve>
  label: Approve;
  [role_purch: Order the material] <block_apply>
  label: Order;
  props: PO;
  desc: The purchase order is approved, then sent to the supplier.;
  [role_purch: Record the receipt] <block_apply>
  label: Receipt;
  desc: The material that arrived goes into material stock.;
elseif (Enough) than
endif
[role_appr: Approve work order] <block_approve>
label: Approve;
desc: On approval, the material for manufacture is reserved.;
[role_mfg: Run the steps] <block_apply>
label: Manufacture;
desc: On the floor tablet, start and complete each step and record quantities and defects. When every step is done, the finished goods go into product stock.;
[role_ship: Confirm delivery] <block_apply>
label: Delivery order;
props: DO,DN;
desc: Confirming creates the delivery note automatically.;
[role_ship: Ship] <block_apply>
label: Ship;
desc: Product stock goes down and the order line becomes 出荷済 (Shipped).;
[role_ship: Mark as delivered] <block_apply>
label: Deliver;
[role_acct: Close the period] <block_money>
label: Closing;
desc: On each customer's closing day, the period's deliveries are gathered.;
[role_acct: Send the invoice] <block_money>
label: Invoice;

desc: An invoice is created from the closing; issue it and send the PDF.;
