[role_sales: Compute a unit price] <block_apply>
label: Price estimate;
desc: Compute an estimated unit price from material, dimensions, and machining conditions, then confirm it once finalized.;
[role_sales: Create a price list] <block_apply>
label: Price list;
props: PRICE_LIST;
desc: Set prices per customer × product. A confirmed price estimate can be used as the base price.;
[role_sales: Issue a quote] <block_apply>
label: Quote;
props: QUOTE;
desc: The unit price is auto-filled from the price list. Submit the issued PDF to the customer.;
[role_partner: Send a purchase order] <block_neutral>
label: Order;
desc: A purchase order arrives by fax, PDF, etc.;
[role_support: Check the order acceptance] <block_apply>
label: Order acceptance;
props: ORDER_ACCEPT;
desc: Reading happens automatically; the price-matching result is also checked here.;
[role_approver: Approve the order acceptance] <block_approve>
label: Approval;
[role_support: Confirm order lines] <block_apply>
label: Confirm lines;
desc: An order line is created per line item and the amount is finalized.;
[role_support: Create a work order] <block_apply>
label: Work order;
props: WORK_ORDER;
desc: Split into from-stock and to-manufacture portions; steps are arranged for the to-manufacture portion.;
[role_approver: Approve the work order] <block_approve>
label: Approval;
[role_mfg: Execute the steps] <block_apply>
label: Production;
desc: Record start/completion and quantity/defects for each step.;
[role_support: Ship with a delivery order] <block_apply>
label: Shipping;
props: SHIPPING;
desc: Shipping reduces stock and changes the order line's status.;
[role_support: Deliver with a delivery note] <block_apply>
label: Delivery;
props: DELIVERY;
[role_billing: Close on the billing closing date] <block_apply>
label: Billing closing;
desc: Deliveries are aggregated by each customer's closing date.;
[role_billing: Send the invoice] <block_apply>
label: Billing;
props: INVOICE;
