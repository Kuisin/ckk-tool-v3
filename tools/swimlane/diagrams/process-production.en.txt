/line/

[role_prod: Match against stock] <block_condition>
label: Stock matching;
desc: Split into the portion that can ship from stock and the portion to newly manufacture.;
if (Product stock) is (Sufficient) than #green
  [role_support: From-stock work order] <block_apply>
  label: From stock;
  props: WORK_ORDER;
  desc: A from-stock work order has a fixed structure (dispense product + optional pre-shipment inspection). Stock is allocated.;
else
  [role_prod: Check material] <block_condition>
  label: Material check;
  desc: Check whether the needed material is sufficient. If not, procure it through the purchasing flow.;
  [role_support: Manufacture work order] <block_apply>
  label: To manufacture;
  props: WORK_ORDER;
  desc: Arrange the steps in order. If a prior work order for the same customer and product exists, it can be copied.;
endif
[role_support: Request approval] <block_apply>
label: Approval request;
desc: Production cannot start until approved. The number of steps is decided by the approval settings.;
if (Approval decision) is (Sent back) than #red
  [role_support: Fix and re-request] <block_reject>
  label: Sent back;
  [loop]
else
  [role_approver: Approve the work order] <block_approve>
  label: Approval;
endif
[role_mfg: Execute the steps] <block_apply>
label: Step execution;
desc: Record start/completion for each step, along with received/good quantity and the defect breakdown. Defects can be reworked via a process branch.;
[role_mfg: Complete the final step] <block_apply>
label: Completion;
[role_system: Receive into product stock] <block_system>
label: Stock receipt;
desc: The completed quantity enters product stock, and the allocation to the order is finalized. Proceed to the shipping flow.;
