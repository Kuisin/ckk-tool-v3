/line/

[role_prod: File a purchase request] <block_apply>
label: Purchase request;
desc: Write the wanted material, quantity, receiving site, requested date, and the reason, then request approval.;
if (Request approval) is (Sent back) than #red
  [role_prod: Fix and re-request] <block_reject>
  label: Sent back;
  desc: Fix the reason or quantity and request again.;
  [loop]
else
  [role_approver: Approve the request] <block_approve>
  label: Request approval;
endif
[role_purchase: Create a purchase order] <block_apply>
label: Purchase order;
desc: Decide the supplier, unit price, and expected receiving date. For urgent or small-amount cases, it can be created directly without going through a request.;
[role_approver: Approve the purchase order] <block_approve>
label: PO approval;
desc: Check the amount and terms before approving. If sent back, fix and request again.;
[role_purchase: Order from the supplier] <block_apply>
label: Order;
desc: Once ordered, it is treated as an expected receipt.;
[role_receiving: Record the receipt] <block_apply>
label: Material receipt;
desc: Record the quantity received, the date, and the receiving site.;
[role_system: Increase the site's stock] <block_system>
label: Stock update;
desc: The recorded receiving site's material stock increases as of the recorded receiving date.;
if (Gap vs. ordered qty) is (Remaining) than #orange
  [role_receiving: Wait for the next receipt] <block_wait>
  label: Split receipt;
  desc: When delivered in parts, record only what arrived; the rest is recorded as a later receipt.;
  [loop]
else
  [role_system: Becomes receipt completed] <block_system>
  label: Receipt completed;
  desc: Once the full ordered quantity has arrived, the purchase order's status becomes "Received".;
endif
[role_prod: Outsourcing starts from a step] <block_memo>
label: (Reference) Outsourcing;
desc: An outsource order is not created from purchasing — selecting "Outsourced" as a work order step's execution location makes it appear in the outsource order list.;
